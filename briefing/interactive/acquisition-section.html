The acquisition record is broader than the large projects

The public-record search identifies 15 acquisition events across 18 site records, September 2016–September 7, 2026: fourteen purchases and one gift. Smaller houses, vacant land, a former clinical building and a donated chapter house sit alongside the large apartment, education, retail and hotel purchases. The count groups shared transactions; it is not a certification that every subsidiary or legal interest has been found.

Why varies by asset. Housing and academic reuse are documented for the Marshall and Marley, and SU explicitly links Skyler to visitor lodging after the Sheraton conversion. Many smaller purchases have no published purchase-time explanation. No reliable asking prices or property-specific appraisals were located. A later tax assessment is not a measure of what a property was worth at purchase.
